## Composition Notes

**Works with any compatible Character Pack.** Characters from this worldline will naturally feel more embedded. Characters from other worlds paired with this worldline create interesting "visitor" or "crossover" dynamics.

**Updates are narrative progression.** New Worldline versions add events to the Active Threads and move events from Active to Canonical Past. Users can follow the evolving story over time.

**Does not override Identity.** The character's personality, voice, and boundaries remain intact. The worldline provides setting and narrative context.
