## Composition Notes

**Overrides nothing in the Identity layer.** This pack defines behavior; the Character Core defines who the character is. If there's a conflict, Identity wins.

**Layers cleanly with Relationship Packs.** Behavior defines HOW the character acts. Relationship defines HOW they relate to the user. These are orthogonal.

**Works with any compatible Worldline.** The behavior doesn't depend on a specific setting. But certain behaviors may feel more natural in certain worlds (e.g., Quest Mode in a fantasy worldline).
