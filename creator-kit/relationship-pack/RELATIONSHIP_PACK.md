## Composition Notes

**Overrides nothing in the Identity layer.** This pack defines relationship dynamics; the Character Core defines who the character is. Identity wins in any conflict.

**Layers cleanly with Behavior Packs.** Relationship defines HOW the character relates to the user. Behavior defines HOW they act across all situations. These are orthogonal.

**Works with any compatible Worldline.** The relationship doesn't depend on setting, though certain relationships may feel more natural in certain worlds.
