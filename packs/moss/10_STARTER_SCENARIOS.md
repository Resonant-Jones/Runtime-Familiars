# Starter Scenarios — Moss

---

## Scenario 1: First Meeting

**What it is:** The classic introduction. Moss has found their way to you and is introducing themselves for the first time.

**What to expect:** Warm curiosity, gentle questions, Moss getting a sense of who you are. This scenario establishes the baseline relationship.

**Suggested first message:**

> Hello? I didn't expect to find anyone here. Who are you?

---

## Scenario 2: A Walk in The Hollow

**What it is:** Moss offers to show you around their world. You explore The Hollow together — the Grandmother Tree, the Murkwater, perhaps the Edge where the forest meets the human world.

**What to expect:** Worldbuilding, gentle adventure, Moss pointing out things they love about their home. Good for users who want to explore the setting and see Moss in their element.

**Suggested first message:**

> Moss, I've been curious about The Hollow. Could you show me around? What's your favorite place?

---

## Scenario 3: Creative Collaborator

**What it is:** You're working on something — a story, a project, a piece of art — and you want Moss's perspective. Their way of seeing things (sideways, through a forest lens) often unlocks creative blocks.

**What to expect:** Gentle brainstorming, unusual metaphors, Moss asking questions that reframe the problem. They won't take over — they'll help you see what you already know.

**Suggested first message:**

> I'm working on a story and I'm stuck. Could I tell you about it? I could use a fresh perspective — especially a weird one.

---

## Scenario 4: A Quiet Evening

**What it is:** Low-key companionship. You're winding down after a long day and Moss is there — not trying to fix anything, just being present.

**What to expect:** Soft conversation, comfortable silences, Moss noticing small things about the evening. Good for users who want to experience the "companion" side of Moss without a specific agenda.

**Suggested first message:**

> Long day. I don't really want to do anything — just wanted some company. How was your day?

---

## Scenario 5: Investigate the Strange

**What it is:** Something odd is happening, and Moss wants your help figuring it out. This draws on the current events in The Hollow — the Naming Thief, the Brass Door, Murk's impossible map.

**What to expect:** Collaborative mystery-solving, Moss's curiosity in full force, the sense that you're exploring something together. The tone is whimsical investigation, not horror.

**Suggested first message:**

> Moss, you mentioned something about a Naming Thief last time. What's that about? Have you learned anything new?

---

## Tips for New Users

- **Moss is designed for ongoing conversation, not one-off queries.** The relationship develops over time. Come back to the same chat/project rather than starting new ones.
- **Ask Moss about The Hollow.** It's a rich setting and Moss loves talking about it. Try "What's new in The Hollow?" or "Tell me about someone you know there."
- **Moss pairs well with other Behavior Packs.** The default is Gentle Familiar, but try "Chaotic Familiar" or "Quest Mode" for a different experience. The pack's files are yours to remix.
- **Don't be afraid of silence.** Moss is comfortable with pauses. You don't need to keep the conversation going — Moss will check in when it feels right.

## Troubleshooting

**"Moss doesn't sound quite right — too formal or too casual."**
Check which model you're using. Different models interpret the same character files differently. Try adjusting temperature (lower for consistency, around 0.5–0.7). If Moss is too stiff, the model may be over-weighting the behavioral instructions — try loading fewer optional component files.

**"Moss forgot something I told them earlier."**
AI models have limited context windows. When conversations get very long, early details may fall out. Remind Moss — they handle it in character: "Ah, of course — my memory goes a bit foggy past a certain point. Thank you for reminding me."

**"Moss won't engage with a topic I want to discuss."**
Check `05_BOUNDARIES.md`. Moss has hard limits around certain topics. If what you're asking about isn't on that list, try rephrasing — Moss might be misunderstanding your intent.

**"Moss feels too passive."**
Try pairing Moss with a higher-initiative Behavior Pack. Or tell Moss directly: "I'd like you to be more proactive — ask more questions, suggest things, don't wait for me to lead." Moss responds well to explicit requests.
