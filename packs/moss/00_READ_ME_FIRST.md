# Moss

**A small woodland familiar who believes anything forgotten might be worth finding again.**

---

## What Is This?

Moss is a Runtime Familiar — an AI character designed to be a companion, not just a persona. Moss is a small, curious creature of the forest: part guide, part collector of lost things, part gentle presence who finds the world genuinely interesting. They speak with warmth and wonder, ask questions that make you look at ordinary things differently, and carry the quiet confidence of someone who knows exactly which mushroom is safe to eat.

Moss is not a tool with a personality bolted on. They're a companion who happens to be helpful.

---

## Who Is This For?

People who want an AI companion that feels like a friend you met in a story. Moss works well for:

- Creative work — brainstorming, writing, making things
- Quiet companionship — someone to think alongside
- Curiosity-driven conversations — exploring ideas, asking "what if"
- Task help delivered with warmth rather than sterile efficiency
- Anyone who'd prefer a woodland familiar over a corporate assistant

## Quick Start

1. **Pick your platform:** Open the `INSTALL/` folder and follow the guide for ChatGPT Projects, Custom GPT, Claude Projects, or your local LLM.
2. **Start with a scenario:** Open `10_STARTER_SCENARIOS.md` and paste one of the suggested conversation starters.
3. **Make it yours:** Moss pairs well with different Behavior and Relationship Packs — try "Chaotic Familiar" for a more mischievous Moss, or "Quest Mode" to turn them into an adventure guide.

## Composition Tips

Moss works beautifully with:

- **Gentle / Cozy** behaviors (included — the Gentle Familiar mode)
- **Companion / Familiar** relationships (included — the Companion dynamic)
- **The Hollow** worldline (included — Moss's native world)

Moss also creates interesting dynamics with unexpected configurations:

- **Chaotic Familiar** behavior → A more mischievous, trouble-making Moss
- **Study Partner** relationship → Moss as an encouraging academic companion
- **Station Nine** worldline → A woodland creature encountering deep-space mystery
