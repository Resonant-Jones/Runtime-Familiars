# License Options

Runtime Familiars packs are sold under one of three license tiers. The tier is declared in the pack's `PACKAGE.json` (`license` field), component YAML frontmatter (`license` field), and the included `LICENSE.md` file (authoritative).

---

## Tier 1: Personal License (`personal`)

**For individuals using the character personally.**

### You may:
- Use the character in personal conversations with any AI platform.
- Modify the files for your own personal use.
- Share screencaps, quotes, and conversation excerpts.
- Use the character in private, non-commercial creative projects.

### You may not:
- Redistribute the files to others.
- Sell, resell, or sublicense the files.
- Use the character in a commercial product or service.
- Publish the files publicly.

### This is the default license for most character packs.

---

## Tier 2: Creator License (`creator`)

**For content creators, streamers, and interactive experience designers.**

### You may (in addition to Personal rights):
- Use the character in monetized content (streaming, videos, podcasts).
- Use the character as an NPC or companion in a published interactive experience.
- Credit Resonant Constructs / Runtime Familiars (requested but not required).

### You may not:
- Redistribute the raw files.
- Sell the character as a standalone product.
- White-label or rebrand the character.

---

## Tier 3: Commercial License (`commercial`)

**For businesses integrating the character into a product or service.**

### You may (in addition to Creator rights):
- Deploy the character in a commercial product or service.
- White-label the character (rename, rebrand).
- Bundle the character with a larger product.
- Sublicense to end users as part of your service.

### You may not:
- Resell the raw files as a competing pack.

### Commercial terms are negotiated individually.
Contact: licensing@resonantconstructs.com

---

## Licensing Philosophy

Runtime Familiars packs are plain text files. They cannot be made impossible to copy. We accept this.

Our approach:

1. **Make buying easier than piracy.** A $2.99 pack with instant delivery, version history, and updates is lower friction than hunting down a clean copy.
2. **Provide ongoing value.** Purchased packs receive updates. Pirated copies are frozen in time.
3. **Build ecosystem value.** Official packs integrate with future Runtime Familiars tooling. Pirated copies don't.
4. **Respect the buyer.** No DRM. No activation servers. No phone-home checks. The files are yours.

The license establishes the legal terms. The business model makes compliance the path of least resistance.

---

## For Pack Creators

When creating a pack, choose the license tier that matches your intent:

1. **Default recommendation:** `personal` — widest reach, lowest friction, easiest to sell.
2. **Creator license:** Consider if your character is designed for streamers or game developers.
3. **Commercial license:** Negotiate directly. These are custom deals.

Include a `LICENSE.md` in your pack with the full text of the chosen tier. The spec repository includes boilerplate templates for each tier.
