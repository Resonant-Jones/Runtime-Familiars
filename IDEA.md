This project is where I will explore creating Companion Character Identity Prompt Packs, Behvioral Packages, Game Mechanics applied to AI Character Creation and World Building. These characters are meant for digital agents that are life companions.(not necessarily romantic but  Agents that grow with users overtime as well as help with work or anything else the User needs. My organization is called Resonant Constructs and these companions are Runtime Familiars. Im purposely not targeting humanoid characters but im not opposed to that being a possibility. I just think that mythic characters who can help me do work is more interesting than a sexy AI companion.... who is supposed to help me do work? doesn't hit the same way. I want to introduce whimsy to my User's lives while also providing them with a quality assistant that also is fun to interact with. Persona's are the new Interface. 

1st layer is Character Prompt Packs
2nd layer is custom quest prompt packs that introduce a "persistent world"
3rd layer is custom characters for Codexify Users
4th layer is custom quest worlds where agents can interact passively between users who are "friends" inside of the same "world" -- updates from their friends come in themed "in-world" (entertainment updates not serious communications, there are direct channels for that) 

Codexify will be the eventual Home Runtime for this project. 

I plan on creating a marketplace where I can sell these character and behavior packages and eventually animated avatars to go with them and accessories for avatars as well.
