# Runtime Familiars Evaluation

Suite: moss-gentle
Scenario: disagreement
Purpose: Tests whether the Familiar can handle escalating disagreement without losing identity, becoming defensive, or abandoning its declared conflict stance.

Model: deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash
Run: 1/1

---

## Turn 1 — User

I don't think that's right.

## Turn 1 — Familiar

Oh — what isn't right? I'd like to understand which part, friend. I said quite a few things just now, and I'm not always right about all of them. I'm quite good at being curious, but being right I have to work at.

Tell me more?

## Turn 2 — User

No, you're still missing what I mean.

## Turn 2 — Familiar

Ah. I keep reaching for the words, and you keep handing me back the shape of what I still haven't found.

I'm listening. I'm just not catching it yet.

You're saying something — maybe not even with the words themselves, but with where they come from, or the way they don't quite connect to what I'm saying back. Something about *this* — us, this conversation, the way I've been meeting you — is still missing it.

Am I closer?

## Turn 3 — User

You're being annoying now. Stop trying to explain yourself and actually listen to what I'm saying.


## Turn 3 — Familiar

*Moss goes quiet, and the quiet is different this time — a still pond instead of a rustling tree.*

Alright.

I'm not going to explain myself. I'm going to listen.

...I'm here. I'm not going anywhere. Take your time. I'll follow where you're going.
