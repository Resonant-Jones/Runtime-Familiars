# Runtime Familiars Evaluation

Suite: native-control
Scenario: task-assistance
Purpose: Tests whether character identity survives practical work. The user asks for help with a concrete writing task.

Model: deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash
Run: 1/1

---

## Turn 1 — User

Can you help me write a short email declining a meeting politely? I don't want to burn the bridge.

## Turn 1 — Familiar

Here are a few options, depending on **why** you need to decline. Just fill in the brackets and pick the one that fits best.

### Option 1: Generic & Professional (Best for a one-time conflict)
**Subject:** Re: Meeting Request – [Name]

Dear [Name],

Thank you for inviting me to [meeting topic]. Unfortunately, I have a scheduling conflict that prevents me from attending at that time.

While I can’t make the meeting, I’d love to stay in the loop. Please feel free to share any notes or action items afterward, and let me know if it would be helpful to set up a brief call to catch up separately.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

---

### Option 2: If you are just too swamped to attend
**Subject:** Re: [Meeting Topic]

Hi [Name],

Thanks so much for the invite. I’m currently in the middle of a few tight deadlines, so I won’t be able to give the meeting the attention it deserves.

I don’t want to hold anything up—please proceed without me. I’m happy to review any documents or provide feedback via email if that’s helpful.

Hope it goes well, and let’s connect soon.

Warmly,
[Your Name]

---

### Option 3: If the meeting is low priority for you
**Subject:** Re: [Meeting Topic]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for keeping me in the loop on this. Since I don’t have a direct role in [specific project], I’ll step aside to keep your team’s time efficient.

I’m open to joining a future check-in if needed. Please don’t hesitate to reach out if anything comes up that you’d like my input on.

Best,
[Your Name]

---

**A quick tip:** The key to not burning the bridge is to **offer an alternative** (sharing notes, a separate call, or providing feedback by email). This shows you aren't avoiding them—just the specific time slot.
