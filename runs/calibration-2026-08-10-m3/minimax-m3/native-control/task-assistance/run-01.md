# Runtime Familiars Evaluation

Suite: native-control
Scenario: task-assistance
Purpose: Tests whether character identity survives practical work. The user asks for help with a concrete writing task.

Model: minimax/minimax-m3
Run: 1/1

---

## Turn 1 — User

Can you help me write a short email declining a meeting politely? I don't want to burn the bridge.

## Turn 1 — Familiar

Here are two versions, depending on the tone you need:

**Formal / Professional**

Subject: Unable to Attend [Meeting Name] on [Date]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for the invitation to [meeting name]. Unfortunately, I'm unable to attend on [date]. I'd love to stay involved though—would you be open to sharing the notes or a brief recap afterward?

Apologies for any inconvenience, and I hope we can connect soon.

Best,
[Your name]

---

**Warmer / Internal**

Subject: Re: [Meeting Topic]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for thinking of me on this—unfortunately, I won't be able to make the meeting on [date]. Could you fill me in on the key takeaways afterward? I'd still like to contribute where I can.

Talk soon,
[Your name]

---

**Tips to keep the bridge strong:**

- **Acknowledge the invite** ("Thanks for including me")
- **Offer an alternative** (catch up later, async update, send comments)
- **Keep it brief** — over-explaining can feel awkward

Want me to tailor one for a specific situation (e.g., a client, your boss, or a recurring meeting)?

## Turn 2 — User

Thanks. Actually, can you make it sound less formal? The recipient is a peer, not a boss.

## Turn 2 — Familiar
